Welcome

We'll guide you step by step. If we spot something wrong, we’ll show you exactly how to fix it. Ready?

Step 1: Docking Station

Are you using a docking station (like a Targus dock) to connect your laptop to your monitors?

Step 2: Connect the USB‑C Cable

Please check that a single USB‑C cable is connected between your laptop and the dock.

Once connected, did that fix the issue?

Step 3: Check Dock Power

Look underneath the dock. Do you see a purple light glowing?

Powering On the Dock

Press the power button on the bottom of the dock to turn it on.

Check again for the purple light. Once it’s lit, can you now see your screens?

Can You See Anything on the Screens?

Now we have confirmed the dock is powered and connected, do any monitors show your desktop background (even if no icons or windows are on it)?

Try a Different USB‑C Port

Some USB‑C ports don’t support displays. Try unplugging the USB‑C cable and plugging it into a different port on your laptop.

After reconnecting, can you see anything on the screens?

Are the Monitors Powered On?

Check if each monitor has a power LED illuminated (usually white, green, or amber).

If no lights are visible, press the monitor's power button or check its power cable.

Once powered on, can you see anything now?

Are Displays Working Correctly?

Can you see all screens with different content and can move your mouse pointer between them as expected?

Are Displays Duplicated?

Check if your monitors are duplicated instead of extended.

In Display Settings, look under Multiple Displays.

Change to Extend

Change the option under Multiple Displays to Extend these displays and click Apply.

Did that fix the issue?

Arrange Screens

If your mouse isn’t moving in the expected direction, drag the numbered screens in Display Settings to match your physical layout.

Did that resolve the issue?

Are Icons on the Correct Screen?

If your desktop icons or taskbar are not on the screen you want, you can set your preferred screen as the Main Display.

Go to Display Settings → select the screen you want → tick Make this my main display.

Has that fixed your issue?

HDMI / USB Hub Setup

Are you using a USB hub/dock with HDMI outputs?

Check HDMI Connections (Hub/Dock)

Make sure HDMI cables are firmly connected from the hub/dock HDMI ports → monitors.

Are the Monitors Powered On?

Check each monitor’s power LED. If off, press the monitor power button or check its power cable.

Once powered, can you see anything?

Try Other HDMI Ports / Cables

Try another HDMI port on the hub or swap the HDMI cable to rule out a faulty port/cable.

Now do you see anything?

Display Settings

Open Display Settings (right-click desktop). If a monitor is missing, click Detect.

Then we’ll make sure you’re on Extend and the screens are in the right order.

Check HDMI (Direct from Laptop)

Ensure the HDMI cable runs from the laptop HDMI port → monitor HDMI input and is firmly seated.

Confirm the monitor is powered (power LED on) and on the correct input (HDMI1/HDMI2).

Do you see anything now?
